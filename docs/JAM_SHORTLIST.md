## Automatic-entry gate
Duff approved automatic entry ONLY if all conditions pass:
- Current playable build earns documented internal 8.5/10 or better, with no critical blockers. Record version/commit, date, review evidence and actual playtest findings. Historical ratings don't transfer to new builds. This is our internal benchmark, not an IGN review or endorsement.
- Free entry, existing game permitted, AI use and appropriate disclosure allowed, theme/platform/dates/licensing satisfied; multiple entries only where explicitly permitted.
- Existing approved public itch listing only. No pricing, branding, listing visibility, paid ads, personal data disclosure or spending changes. No additional obligations without separate approval. Skip uncertainty and duplicate entries.
- Respect game-specific holds (including Chaos until it earns 8.5), paused projects and paused reviews. Never publish a game merely to enter a jam.
- Verify submission and log event, game, build, review evidence, submission URL, deadline and any approved obligations.

## 8.5 to 10/10: discussion roadmap
8.5 is the minimum gate, not the target. For every candidate, evaluate the first minute (clear hook, quick playable action); one signature mechanic worth telling a friend about; responsive controls and feedback; invisible polish (UI, sound, loading, accessibility, stability and phone performance); emotional payoff; desire to replay voluntarily. Record concrete observations and prioritize the biggest gaps before adding more features. Test with fresh players; don't inflate scores or imply external endorsement.
